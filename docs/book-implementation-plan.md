# Single-book implementation

Implemented: editable book settings; ordered chapters and perspectives; separate
private drafts and published text; author sessions; one-time whole-book purchases;
email reader sign-in; centralized paid-text checks; explicit free chapters;
on-demand proofreading with individual approval.

Chapter 1 starts free. Other chapters start paid. A purchase includes future paid
chapters. Price remains unset until the author chooses it. Optional contributions,
subscriptions, per-chapter purchases, and private media are deferred.

See [book.md](book.md) for setup, operating instructions, and validation.
